Privacy policy — agent-server-backup

Last updated: 22 September 2026

agent-server-backup is a private tool used only by its owner to store encrypted backups of the owner's own server in the owner's own Google Drive.

What data it accesses

It uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. This lets it see and manage only the files it has created itself. It cannot read any other files in Google Drive, email, contacts or any other Google data.

What it does with the data

It uploads backup files, checks that they arrived, and deletes backups older than the retention period. Backups are encrypted before upload.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Nothing is collected from anyone other than the owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
